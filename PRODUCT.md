# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Sonarr/Radarr users with moderate technical experience, setting up the extension in a desktop browser to connect to their self-hosted services.

## Product Purpose
Make setup fast and reliable so users can add one or more *arr servers, understand permission prompts, and finish configuration without fighting popup constraints.

## Brand Personality
easy, minimalist, functional

## Anti-references
None specified.

## Design Principles
- Make the setup flow resilient and predictable.
- Explain permissions clearly at the moment they matter.
- Keep the surface focused on the next action.
- Support adding multiple apps without repeating steps.
- Prefer clarity over density.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
No specific requirements stated.
